# Features

## Character Creation

### ARM INE???

**Armor** is the chance a bullet goes through, and there are differnt  damage resistances

**Reflex** is speed for attack and movement abilities.

**Muscle** is the power of melee attacks and throws and inventory capacity.

**Intel** is the ability to gather and use pieces of information. like Qud these can be read but really are just items that unlock access. and also allow you to upgrade abilities. 

**Nerve** allows you to use special abilities and Buffs, sometimes movement related, related to special items and character modifications. 

**Ego** modifies influence on people in the world. through certain negotiations, you gain allies, pacify enemies, and get deals.

## Game play 

### Combat


You always like "shoot" a projectile, although melee its a large and short range projectile. 
You can dash and some items are abilites allow you to fly over water and other terrain, or dive and swim quickly. 

**Armor** is the chance a bullet effect health, and there are different damage resistances

**Reflex** is speed for attack and movement abilities.

**Muscle**  Determines accuracy with stronger weapons. (the power of ranged attacks depends on the weapon).  Also determines throws and inventory capacity.


**Ego**
You can engage in communication although its all unintelligible sounds and gestures, called signals. 
some signals are positive, and some you project strength, and intimidate. 

**Nerve**
In the world there is mutant powers, like fire breath, and acid/poison/slime/spider-web spit. 
There is rapid fungal and botanic growth. 

There is psychic-abilities that are limited by a dust like gas that is inhaled. 
They control, electro-magnetic fields. 

**Intel**
There is cybernetics that allow you control robots and, and advanced weapons, and scanners. 

These will be like systems and in the programming you are creating material that interacts with each other.

### AI 

The NPC Ai is has a range of attributes

- cliquey - groups stay together, often around leader
- scared - stay behind objects form the player
- aggerssive - run toward player
- flank - keep distance, use projectiles
- flee - run off and then return (mixture of scared and flank)


often they are passive and curious.
