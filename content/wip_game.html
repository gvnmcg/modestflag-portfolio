Character Creation

Armor is the chance a bullet goes through, and there are differnt damage resistances

Reflex is speed for attack and movement abilities.

Muscle is the power of melee attacks and throws and inventory capacity.

Intel is the ability to gather and use pieces of information. like Qud these can be read but really are just items that unlock access. and also allow you to upgrade abilities.

Nerve allows you to use special abilities and Buffs, sometimes movement related, related to special items and character modifications.
