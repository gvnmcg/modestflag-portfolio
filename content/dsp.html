DSP

I am interested in Digital Signal Processing and its scientific applications.

For Example here is a Guitar Audio processing project Guitar Chord Detector

This single file project that takes guitar audio from a microphone (web media) and uses the FFT (Fast Fourier Transform) to determine frequencies and thus Notes. The Notes are visualized on a fretboard.

The project was a tool to remember guitar fretting after creating a recording. For myself and for students.
